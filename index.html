About Me

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, specializing in natural language processing, machine learning, and distributed systems. Before grad school, I spent 5 years at Target Corporation as a software engineer, where I worked on large-scale search systems, real-time data pipelines, and low-latency production services used by millions of users.

My work spans from fine-tuning LLMs like LLaMA 2 for Named Entity Recognition to architecting scalable backend systems using Spark, Kafka, and Kubernetes. I enjoy building practical ML tools—like a mini deep learning framework in CUDA C++ and multilingual tokenizers for factual knowledge retrieval—and thrive at the intersection of systems and machine learning.

Beyond my professional work, I actively lead learning groups, mentor aspiring programmers, and enjoy exploring emerging ideas and reading cutting-edge research papers and building practical systems inspired by them.
